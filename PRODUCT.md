# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Owner of two Android devices who needs to remotely monitor and control the secondary device. The user values clarity, reliability, and ease of use.

## Product Purpose
A monitoring dashboard for a Telegram-controlled bot that provides real-time status updates, activity logs, and management of scheduled reminders. It serves to give the user confidence that the background bot is active and performing correctly.

## Brand Personality
Professional, Clean, and Informative. It should feel like a reliable utility tool—no fluff, high utility, and organized.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered SaaS dashboards with unnecessary charts.
- Overly "cutesy" or consumer-grade apps with heavy gradients.
- Generic "AI-generated" layouts with inconsistent spacing.

## Design Principles
- **Utility First**: Every element on the screen must serve a clear monitoring or control purpose.
- **Immediate Clarity**: A glance should tell the user if the bot is healthy (Online/Offline) or if errors occurred.
- **Hierarchical Depth**: Important stats (Bot Status) are prominent, while secondary logs are neatly organized.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for readability in various lighting conditions.
- Clear status indicators (icons + text) for color-blind accessibility.
- Clean typography for easy scanning of log entries.
